AI-Powered Near-Expiry Food Redistribution Platform

Redistributes near-expiry groceries via dynamic pricing and a micro-currency system (GroCoin). Handles user verification, monthly credit allocation, item reservation, and pickup scheduling. LinkPlease

Comment-to-DM Automation Platform

Flask webhook service automating Instagram comment-to-DM replies. SQLite unique constraints guarantee exactly-once delivery per user/rule pair, backed by a multi-threaded architecture (processor, sender, poller) with rate-limit handling, exponential-backoff retries, and per-attempt idempotency keys — safely processing 500-event bursts. Secured with HMAC-SHA256 webhook signature verification, with live accuracy stats exposed via a REST API.

  • Flask
  • SQLite
  • HMAC-SHA256
  • Multithreading
